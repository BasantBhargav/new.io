Our Mission

In India, millions of people still carry paper reports in plastic bags, wait hours at hospitals without knowing their turn, and lose critical medical history when switching doctors. We built MediVault to change that.

MediVault is your personal digital health companion — a single place to store reports, book appointments, track your queue, get prescriptions, and receive lab results. For doctors, hospitals, and labs, it's a smarter way to serve patients and grow their practice.
